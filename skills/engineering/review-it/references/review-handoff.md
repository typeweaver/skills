# Review Handoff Contract

Complete this factual, concise contract before delegating an independent review.
It orients the reviewer without steering the verdict: state verifiable facts,
not persuasion, and expect every claim to be checked against the repository.
Link durable artifacts by path. Omit irrelevant fields, but state evidence gaps.

## Assignment

- **Repository:** <repository or worktree>
- **Candidate:** <exact base and head, staged diff, or explicit file boundary>
- **Excluded:** <unrelated working-tree changes>
- **Instructions:** <applicable repository guidance>

## Intent

- **Feature context:** <overall feature and why it exists>
- **Candidate outcome:** <what this specific slice should achieve>
- **Acceptance evidence:** <what proves this slice complete>
- **Goal and plan:** <paths or durable references>

## Decisions

- **Settled decisions:** <decision, rationale, and important tradeoff>
- **Plan deviations:** <material deviations discovered during implementation>
- **Open uncertainty:** <remaining uncertainty without an expected verdict>

## Review focus

- **Requested feedback:** <specific questions or sensitive areas>
- **Expert perspectives:** <required, forbidden, selected, and why>

## Evidence

- **Checks run:** <actual commands or observations>
- **Results:** <verified outcomes>
- **Not verified:** <known evidence gaps>

Do not include the implementation conversation, an expected verdict, or the
author's defense.
